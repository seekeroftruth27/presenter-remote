## [v1.1.0] — 2026-10-12

Changes from the music director's review, sized for an iPad on stage.

### Added
- **Reorder a playlist**: drag a song by its **≡** grip and let go where it
  should be.

### Changed
- **Remote tab:**
  - Bigger tabs and header text.
  - **PREV / NEXT** in large bold capitals, both the same size, at the bottom.
  - **Clear slide** is a small button next to the zoom slider.
  - The slide grid jumps to the live slide instead of gliding.
  - The Live picture no longer flashes text between slides.
- **Playlist tab:**
  - Taller playlist picker and bigger text.
  - The arrangement sits on the same line as the song.
  - Bigger ▶ and remove buttons; remove is a trash can.
  - **+ Add from library** is bigger and green.
- **Announcements are read-only:** items under an "Announcements" header are
  greyed out and can't be played, moved or removed.
- **Macros tab** shows only Speaking, Welcome, Music & Lyrics, Youth Music and
  Kid's Lyrics, as bigger buttons. Any it can't find are named at the top.
- **↻ (reload)** buttons are about twice the size.

### Removed
- **Clear media** and **Clear all**: the stage team only needs Clear slide.
- **▶ Start** for a whole playlist. **▶** on each item still puts that item on
  screen.
